A
Adam Scrivner
Founder & CEO at Free and Forward
We classified 557 real job listings across 55 US markets. One question: how much of the job market is actually open to someone with a record? 59% were open. 17% needed a closer look before applying. 7% were barred by law. Before we wired a national job data source into Free and Forward, I wanted to know what our users would actually face. Not what a vendor says in a sales call. Real listings, read one by one. Three things stood out: 1. Most postings never mention a record at all. The written barrier is smaller than people assume. 2. The real friction sits later: the background check, the application question, and the gap on the resume. 3. Employers who do hire fairly rarely say so in the posting. You find them by looking, not by searching. That is why our resume builder treats gaps honestly instead of hiding them, and why job matching in the app is weighted toward employers who actually hire people coming home. If you run a reentry program or sit on a workforce board, message me and I will send you the full breakdown. #reentry #fairchancehiring #secondchance
